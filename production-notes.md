- There are many fragments and inconclusively attributed poems in Poe’s corpus. This collection is primarily based on the 1903 collection by P. F. Collier and Son. The Gutenberg transcription was of a slightly earlier collection, so has been updated to match the scans.

- Poems from the scans that were marked as “Doubtful” and nowadays are not regarded as authored by Poe have been removed.

- The poem “Stanzas” was originally untitled, and called in the transcription / scans “In Youth I Have Known One” after the first line. It’s more commonly known nowadays as “Stanzas” so I’ve updated it; see https://www.eapoe.org/works/info/pp018.htm.
